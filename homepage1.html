Kagiso Youth Development Trust

Kagiso Youth DevelopmentTrust quips the youth aged 16-25 with mentorship , digital literacy and business skills to unlock their potential.

Who are we

Founded in 2016 in Soweto,Johannesburg , the Trust began as an after-school homework club and has since grown into a registered non-profit organisation , offering mentorship, digital literacy and business skills training to young people in the community.
