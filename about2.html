About Us

Brief History

Thuli's Home Bakery is small-scale business that start in 2024 in Newcastle. Thuli Ntuli the owner of the business started it with baking celebration cakes in her home kitchen.Word of mouth grew the business steadily and now it deliveries all around South Africa .

Mission statement

To bake high-quality , beautifully designed cakes and treats for every occasion , using fresh locally sourced ingredients and great customer service.

Vision statement

To become Newcastle's most loved home bakery , known for creativity , consistency and community connection.
