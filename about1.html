Brief History

Kagiso Youth Development Trust was founded in 2016 in Soweto,Johannesburg by a group of former teachers who were concerned about the high unemployment of youth in the area .It started out as an after-school homework club and has now grown into a registered NPO that offers membership , digital literacy and small-business skills workshops to the youth aged 16-25.

Mission statement

To empower the younger generation that come from under-resourced communities with skills , confidence and help them network so that they can have access to further education and sustainable employment.

Vision statemnet

A generation of empowered South Africans who are able to shape their own futures and give back to their communities.
